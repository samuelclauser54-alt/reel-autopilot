ChatGPT was never programmed with rules. It learned by rolling downhill. 🏔️⬇️

Every AI (ChatGPT, image generators, self-driving cars) learns with ONE idea from calculus: gradient descent.

1️⃣ A neural network is a function with knobs (GPT-3 has 175 billion).
2️⃣ The "loss" measures how wrong it is.
3️⃣ The derivative points uphill, so step the other way.
4️⃣ The chain rule finds the slope for every knob at once (backpropagation).
5️⃣ Repeat a few million times.
