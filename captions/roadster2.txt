The flying Roadster doesn't burn anything. It just throws air, really fast. 💨

The physics of the flying Roadster, Part 2: how air can shove a 2-tonne car.

1️⃣ Newton's 3rd law: push air backward, the air pushes the car forward
2️⃣ Thrust = (kg thrown per second) × (how fast it leaves): F = ṁ·vₑ
3️⃣ Air squeezed to ~300 atmospheres (scuba-tank pressure) can leave at up to ~700 m/s, about twice the speed of sound
4️⃣ 30,000 N ÷ 700 m/s ≈ 43 kg of air per second, a small bedroom's worth (≈36 m³) every second
5️⃣ SpaceX already does this: Falcon 9 boosters steer with cold-gas (nitrogen) thrusters

Tesla showed compressed-air thrusters at the unveiling. The 300 atm, 700 m/s (an ideal best case) and 30 kN are my estimates, used to show the physics.
