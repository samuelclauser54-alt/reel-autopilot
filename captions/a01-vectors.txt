Today: vectors and norms, the object every AI model is built from.

What you'll learn:
→ A vector is a list of numbers and also an arrow: (3, 4) means 3 right, 4 up
→ Adding vectors = adding matching entries: (3, 4) + (1, −2) = (4, 2), tip to tail
→ Multiplying by a number scales every entry; a negative flips the direction
→ The norm (length): ‖x‖ = √(x₁² + x₂² + … + xₙ²), so ‖(3, 4)‖ = 5 and ‖(1, 2, 2)‖ = 3
→ Dividing by the norm gives a unit vector: same direction, length 1

Real example: the word "king" in the GloVe model is a list of 50 real numbers with norm 5.37. AI compares words by direction (length-1 vectors), and weight decay adds λ‖w‖² to the loss to keep weights small.
