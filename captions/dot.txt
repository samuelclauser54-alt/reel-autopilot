Every word ChatGPT writes = 175 billion copies of ONE tiny operation. 🤯

The math inside AI, Part 1: the dot product.

1️⃣ Multiply matching numbers, then add: (2,1)·(3,4) = 2×3 + 1×4 = 10
2️⃣ Geometrically: a·b = |a| |b| cos θ. It measures how much two arrows point the same way
3️⃣ Same direction → big. Perpendicular → 0. Opposite → negative
4️⃣ A neuron is a dot product: inputs · weights
5️⃣ Recommendation feeds do it too: your taste vector · each video's vector, highest score plays next

The 175 billion is real: GPT-3 has 175B weights, and generating one word takes roughly one multiply-and-add per weight. The neuron and feed numbers are small examples, computed exactly.
