How AI turns words into meaning:

1️⃣ Every word becomes a list of numbers: a vector, an arrow in space (GPT-3 uses 12,288 numbers per word).
2️⃣ Training pulls similar words together: animals in one region, food in another, countries in another.
3️⃣ Directions carry meaning: king − man + woman lands closest to QUEEN. Paris − France + Italy = Rome. Nobody programmed that.
4️⃣ Attention: each word looks at its neighbors and pulls in their meaning, so "bank" next to "river" drifts toward shore, and next to "cash" toward money.
5️⃣ Stack that 96 layers deep and you get GPT.

Every word position in this video is a real projection of real word vectors (GloVe). The king/queen and Paris/Rome results are the actual nearest words.
