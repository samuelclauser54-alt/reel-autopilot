Your inbox kills spam before you ever see it. Here's the math doing it. 📧🗑️

Logistic regression, the classic classifier:

1️⃣ Take one clue: how many exclamation marks the email has.
2️⃣ A straight line through the data is useless: it shoots above 1 and below 0.
3️⃣ So squash it. The sigmoid σ(z) = 1/(1+e⁻ᶻ) bends any number into a probability between 0 and 1.
4️⃣ How does it know it's wrong? Cross-entropy: cost = −log(probability you gave the truth). Say 0.9 for spam → tiny cost. Say 0.01 → the cost explodes.
5️⃣ Gradient descent slides the curve until the total cost is as low as it goes.

This is real: I trained it on 4,600 real emails. One clue alone: 76% right. All 57 clues: 94%.
