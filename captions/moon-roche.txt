The Moon is drifting away from us at 3.8 cm a year. So what if it went the other way? 🌕

2× closer → tides 8× stronger
10× closer → tides 1,000× stronger
18,381 km → Earth's tidal pull rips it apart

That line is called the Roche limit. It's also why Saturn's rings never clumped back into a moon.
