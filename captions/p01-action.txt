A thrown ball "chooses" its path by making one number as small as possible.

That number is the action: add up (kinetic energy − potential energy) over the whole flight.

Real numbers in this video (1 kg ball, 2 s flight, 10 m across):
• the real arc: action = −7.0 J·s
• straight across: 25.0
• twice as high: 25.0
• every height in between traces a valley whose bottom is exactly the real throw

Minimising over whole paths (calculus of variations) gives back F = ma, and the same principle gives Snell's law, Kepler orbits and the path integral of quantum mechanics.

Strictly, nature picks a stationary path (slope zero), not always the minimum. We'll get to that.
