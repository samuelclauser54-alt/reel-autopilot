This AI got a perfect score. And it's completely useless. 💯🚫

Overfitting, the number one trap in machine learning:

1️⃣ Training = find the curve with the smallest squared error.
2️⃣ A straight line is too simple. It misses the bend.
3️⃣ Give it 12 knobs and it hits every single point. Error: zero.
4️⃣ Then show it data it's never seen… way off. It memorized the noise instead of learning the pattern.
5️⃣ The fix (ridge regression): make big weights cost something. Add λ·Σw². The weights shrink from ~400 to ~4 and the wiggles vanish.

Every curve in this video is a real least-squares fit I ran on real noisy data.

Same with practice tests: memorize the answers and a perfect score means nothing.
