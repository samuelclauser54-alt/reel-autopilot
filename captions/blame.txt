GPT-3 has 175 billion knobs. When it's wrong, how does it know which ones to blame? 🔍

Backpropagation, the algorithm behind every neural network (Math of AI, Ep. 2):

1️⃣ Shrink the network to just 2 knobs: a number goes in, gets multiplied, bent (tanh), multiplied again.
2️⃣ It should output 1. It outputs 0.46. Way off.
3️⃣ Nudge knob 1 and watch the ripple: each step scales the change by its local slope.
4️⃣ Multiply those slopes together and you get knob 1's exact "blame". That's the chain rule from calc.
5️⃣ Run it backwards and EVERY knob gets its blame in one sweep, instead of 175 billion separate tests.

This is real: I checked the chain-rule answer by actually nudging the knob by 0.001, and it matches. Then gradient descent trains it until the error hits zero.
