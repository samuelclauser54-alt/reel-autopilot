How does ChatGPT know what "it" means? One formula. 🧠

"The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big." What's "it"?

1️⃣ Every word becomes a vector, a long list of numbers.
2️⃣ The word "it" sends out a question (a query). Every other word holds up a key.
3️⃣ Dot the query with each key: a big dot product means "these two match."
4️⃣ Softmax turns the scores into weights that add to 1, so "it" looks mostly at trophy.
5️⃣ Change one word ("too small") and the same formula now points at suitcase.
6️⃣ Then it mixes in those words' values, so "it" carries their meaning.

That's attention: softmax(QKᵀ/√d)·V. GPT-3 does it with 96 heads in each of 96 layers.
