Every matrix has secret directions it can't turn. ↗️

Eigenvectors, explained visually:

1️⃣ A matrix moves every point in space at once.
2️⃣ Almost every arrow gets knocked off its line…
3️⃣ …but a few stay on their line. They only stretch or shrink. Those are eigenvectors: Av = λv.
4️⃣ λ (the eigenvalue) is the stretch factor. To find it, slide λ until A − λI squashes space flat: det(A − λI) = 0.
5️⃣ The wild part: apply the matrix again and again, and every arrow swings toward the biggest eigenvector (power iteration).

That's how Google ranked the internet: the web is a giant matrix, and PageRank is its top eigenvector.
