The new Roadster drove upside down, and stayed stuck to the ceiling even when it stopped. The secret is the sky. 🌍

The physics of the flying Roadster, Part 4: how it drives upside down.

1️⃣ Race-car wings make downforce ∝ v², so at 0 mph they make nothing
2️⃣ The Roadster uses fans to suck air out from under the car, so it works even parked
3️⃣ Outside air now pushes harder than the thin air left in the gap: F = ΔP · A
4️⃣ Holding ~20,000 N over ~7 m² of floor needs ΔP = F/A ≈ 2,800 Pa, under 3% of normal air pressure
5️⃣ The atmosphere already presses on the car with ~709,000 N (≈72 tonnes of force). The fans just remove a sliver of it from one side

The fan-suction system and holding upside down while stationary come from Tesla's unveiling. The 2-tonne mass and 7 m² floor are my estimates, used to show the physics.
